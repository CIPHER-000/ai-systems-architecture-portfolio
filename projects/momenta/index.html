Architecture Overview

Momenta implements a grounded AI architecture: the LLM is never the source of truth — approved supply data is. A conversational concierge surface sits above intelligence layers for behavioral memory, group-aware recommendations, and a background supply intelligence pipeline. The system optimizes for deciding, not searching — turning fragmented discovery into trusted, contextual experience curation.
